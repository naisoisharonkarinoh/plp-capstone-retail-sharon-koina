# Executive Summary: Supermarket Sales and Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 1. Big Idea

This project analyses synthetic supermarket transaction data to demonstrate how data analytics can help retail managers understand sales performance, compare branches, identify popular product categories, and make informed business decisions.

## 2. Business Problem

Supermarket managers need reliable information about revenue, transaction activity, product performance, and customer purchasing patterns. Without organized analysis, it can be difficult to compare branches, identify trends, and decide where improvements may be needed.

## 3. Data and Methods

The project uses a synthetic dataset containing 50,000 transactions, 20 products, five supermarket branches, and 5,000 customer records.

The analysis uses Python, pandas, SQL, SQLite, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualizations, and a statistical test comparing transaction values between Nairobi and Mombasa.

## 4. Key Findings

The notebooks calculate overall revenue, monthly sales, branch performance, category performance, and customer spending patterns. They also compare average transaction values between Nairobi and Mombasa.

The final numerical findings and statistical test results should be inserted here after checking the outputs in `02_eda.ipynb` and `03_analysis.ipynb`.

## 5. Recommendations

- Monitor monthly revenue to identify changes in sales performance.
- Compare branch performance before allocating budgets and resources.
- Review product category performance when planning inventory and promotions.
- Use customer spending analysis to guide customer engagement strategies.
- Validate findings against real business data before making operational decisions.

## 6. Expected Business Impact

The project demonstrates how structured data analysis can support evidence-based decisions, improve sales monitoring, and help managers investigate opportunities for better inventory and resource allocation.

## 7. Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and does not represent actual supermarket sales or real customer behaviour. Patterns and statistical results are illustrative only. Recommendations must be validated using real business data before implementation.

## 8. Tools Used

Python, pandas, SQLite, SQL, Jupyter/Google Colab, Matplotlib, and statistical analysis.
